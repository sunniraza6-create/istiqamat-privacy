Privacy Policy

Istiqamat — Daily Habit Tracker & Planner

Istiqamat is designed to keep your personal routine information on your device. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

1. Local storage

Your habits, tasks, completion records, streaks, XP and profile settings are stored locally on your device. Istiqamat does not require a user account and does not use its own cloud database or cloud-sync service.

2. Health Connect and sleep data

If you choose to connect Health Connect, Istiqamat requests read access to sleep-session data. The app uses permitted sleep records to display sleep information and related summaries. Sleep information processed by Istiqamat is stored locally on your device and is not sent by Istiqamat to its own servers.

3. Tracking and advertising

Istiqamat does not include third-party advertising networks, third-party tracking SDKs or analytics telemetry.

4. Backups and sharing

If you export a backup, Istiqamat creates a JSON file. The file is saved or shared using the destination you select. Once exported or shared, that file is handled according to the destination's own privacy practices. Keep backup files secure because they may contain your personal app data.

5. Your choices and deleting data

You can manage Health Connect access in Android's Health Connect settings. You can use the app's backup and restore features to manage your records. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it may remove locally stored information, so export a backup first if you want to keep it.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Istiqamat's features or data practices change. The latest version should be made available with the app's store listing.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer: sunniraza6@gmail.com.
